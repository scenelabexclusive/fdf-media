Harvyn Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-09.

Harvyn is a private publishing tool operated by Scene Lab. It is used only to publish video content to social media accounts owned by Scene Lab (including Fever Dream Fiends). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its operator.

What it accesses

With the account owner's authorization, Harvyn requests permission to read basic profile information (display name, username) and to upload or publish videos to the authorized account. It does not read messages, followers, or other users' data.

What it stores

Access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer, in files excluded from version control. Harvyn keeps a local log of what it published (post identifiers, timestamps, captions). Nothing is sent to any third party other than the platform being published to.

What it does not do

Harvyn does not sell, share, or transfer any data. It does not use cookies or analytics. It does not collect data from anyone other than the account owner who authorized it.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's connected-apps settings. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens immediately.
